How to Choose the Right Area

Haad Rin is best for Full Moon and nightlife. Thong Nai Pan feels more premium and scenic. Baan Tai gives you easier access around the island. If you want peace, look for quieter bays and beachfront resorts away from the main party zones.

For Full Moon Party

Stay near Haad Rin if nightlife is your priority. It is the easiest way to walk back after the party instead of paying late-night transport prices.

For Couples & Honeymoon

Choose a quiet beachfront resort in Thong Nai Pan or the east coast. You get better views, more privacy and a more premium atmosphere.

For Easy Island Access

Baan Tai and nearby areas are practical for scooter access, restaurants, beach clubs and easier movement between the north and south of the island.

Quick Stay Advice

Where should I stay for the Full Moon Party?

Haad Rin is still the most practical area if the party is the main reason for your trip. Staying nearby means less stress, cheaper transport and a much easier return after the event.

Which area is best if I want a quieter holiday?

Look at the east coast and more secluded beachfront bays. These areas feel more premium, less chaotic and more suitable for couples or longer relaxing stays.

Should I stay near nightlife or rent a scooter?

If nightlife is your main goal, stay closer to it. If you want more flexibility and quieter surroundings, choose a better hotel in a calmer area and move around the island during the day.
